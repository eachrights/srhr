Every sub-programme: percentage change

Small lines can swing a lot in percentage terms, so read this together with the tables below.

Free Primary Education is KSh 7.0 billion in the Treasury Budget Statement [1] but KSh 16.5 billion in the Budget Brief [3]. The two documents appear to count different things, so the figures should not be combined.

Source for all tables in this section: Basic Education Sub-Sector Budget Brief FY2026/27 [3], pages 6 and 7 as shown under each table. Totals above are the sum of the lines shown. The Budget Brief's printed primary education total for 2026/27 is KSh 22,625 million, which is KSh 59 million lower than the sum of its lines (the adult and continuing education line appears to be left out), so the change shown here is an 11.2% fall against 11.5% in the Brief.
